Goal Setting Frameworks

I use two complementary frameworks to ensure my goals are meaningful, achievable, and aligned with my values.

SMART Model
  • Specific — clearly defined goals, not vague wishes
  • Measurable — progress can be tracked and verified
  • Achievable — realistic given my current skills and situation
  • Relevant — aligned with my data career aspirations
  • Time-bound — with a clear deadline to maintain focus
My SMART Goal: To secure a Data Analyst internship within the next 2 to 3 months, by continuously strengthening my profile through skill development and real-world projects. I am currently building additional projects and completing certifications to make my portfolio more competitive — with the target of submitting applications to relevant organisations and securing a position before the end of August 2026.
SAFE Model
  • Soul-driven — goals aligned with my passion for data and problem solving
  • Affirmative — stated positively, focusing on what I want to achieve
  • Focused — directed at one clear desired outcome at a time
  • Evidenced — I will know clearly when I have achieved them
My SAFE Goal: I am confidently building the data skills, real-world experience, and professional credentials needed to become a skilled Data Analyst — and grow from there into a Data Scientist or Data Engineer within 2 to 3 years.
