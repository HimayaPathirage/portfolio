Anuradhapura Traffic Congestion Index (ATI) — Research Study
👥 Team Research Project
Mathematics Seminar — Rajarata University  ·  2026  ·  Statistical Research
Developed the first empirical traffic congestion index for Anuradhapura UNESCO World Heritage City, Sri Lanka — using a Travel Time Ratio (TTR) based Anuradhapura Traffic Index (ATI)
Built an automated data pipeline using Google Maps Directions API, sampling 19 road segments across the heritage precinct at 15-minute intervals — collecting 27,966 total observations
Applied one-way ANOVA and Tukey HSD statistical analysis to quantify how Poya (full moon) public holidays impact traffic congestion across different day types
Key counterintuitive finding: Saturday (arrival day) is the true peak congestion day — not Poya Monday — as on-foot worship by pilgrims actually suppresses vehicle demand below the normal baseline (p < 0.001)
R ANOVA Tukey HSD Google Maps API Statistical Analysis Research Team Project
